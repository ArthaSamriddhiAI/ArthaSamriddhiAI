# Character Bible: Priya and Dr. Arvind Raghavan (Archetype 07)

**Status:** Active reference for cluster 6 curation; informs case_arch07_a (PA) and case_arch07_b (SCEN).

## Demographic and Structural Facts

Priya Raghavan (45) is a senior pharma executive; she is VP Strategy at Sun Pharma where she has worked for 14 years. Sun Pharma's IPO in 2024 vested her ESOPs at significant scale (Rs 28 Cr paper at current market price). Dr. Arvind Raghavan (47) is a healthcare entrepreneur; he founded Hyderabad-based Apex Diagnostics chain in 2015 (now 12 centres across South India) which generates Rs 3.1 Cr/year in net business earnings to the family.

The Raghavans live in Hyderabad (Banjara Hills) in a self-occupied home (Rs 4.8 Cr, no loan). Two children: a 13-year-old daughter (Class VIII, Hyderabad Public School) and a 10-year-old son (Class V, same school). Education corpus structured priority: target Rs 6 Cr by 2038.

Cultural context: Tamil-Brahmin (Priya, formative context Chennai) and Telugu-Brahmin (Arvind, formative context Hyderabad). Both first-generation urban professionals; Priya's parents are retired teachers from Chennai; Arvind's parents are retired bureaucrats in Vizag. Both sets are financially independent.

Tax structure: individual filers (joint household). Sun Pharma ESOP is in Priya's name only; Apex Diagnostics is structured as a partnership (Arvind 70 percent; rotating partners 30 percent).

Liquid net worth: Rs 23.2 Cr aggregate. Composition: Rs 14.3 Cr Sun Pharma ESOP (paper net of LTCG if liquidated); Rs 5 Cr existing liquid corpus across MFs and FDs; Rs 0.8 Cr cash + Rs 3.1 Cr from Arvind's business earnings deployed.

## Stated Risk Tolerance and Horizon

Priya stated as Moderate-Aggressive (closer to Moderate); 10-12 year horizon to peak retirement (early 50s). Arvind stated as Aggressive; longer horizon (15-20 years).

The household mandate (jointly negotiated) settled on Moderate-Aggressive bucket overall, with explicit acknowledgement that Sun Pharma ESOP creates concentration the household has agreed to address over 18-24 months.

Stated horizon: 12-15 years operational.

## Revealed Behavioural Patterns

Priya's pattern: methodical, slow-deciding, conservative-leaning relative to stated. She held her Sun Pharma ESOP through the 18-month pre-IPO uncertainty (2022-2024) when peers were exiting; her holding pattern signals high company loyalty plus inertia. Post-IPO, she has been deliberate about diversification but slower than her advisor (Amit Sharma) initially expected.

She has revealed behavioural pattern of "I'll move when it makes clear sense" rather than "let me explore options". She values structured analysis. She does not engage with peer-network signals; she relies on advisor + structured data.

Arvind's pattern: more entrepreneurial; willing to consider varied product types. He has separately considered Cat II PE for his business reinvestment (separate from household corpus); the household corpus is more conservative because of Priya's preference dominance.

The household's revealed dynamic: Priya's Moderate preference tends to dominate joint decisions; Arvind defers to her on personal-corpus matters; he reserves his Aggressive instincts for business reinvestment.

They have demonstrated: 12-year SIP discipline, no panic during 2022 drawdown, tax-efficient structuring of ESOP exit windows (engaged tax CA for FY26-27 sequencing), responsive engagement with structured advisor analysis.

## Family Dynamics

Priya is the primary financial decision-maker for the household corpus (her ESOP is the dominant asset). Arvind contributes his business earnings to the household corpus and weighs in on major decisions but accepts Priya's lead.

Children are too young to be involved. Education corpus is structured.

Priya's parents (Chennai, both 72) are retired teachers; modest pensions; the Raghavans contribute approximately Rs 4 L/year aggregate. Not a structural drain. Medical contingency for Priya's mother (recent diabetes complications) is on the radar; not yet a portfolio decision.

Arvind's parents (Vizag, both 70) are retired bureaucrats; pensions adequate; no flow.

Arvind's two siblings (one in US, one in Hyderabad) are not entangled with the household corpus.

Apex Diagnostics has 4 partners (Arvind 70 percent, three rotating physicians 10 percent each); business reinvestment decisions are at partnership level, separate from household corpus. The partners are not a household corpus consideration.

The household has a domestic financial advisor (Amit Sharma) plus a tax CA (separate). Priya occasionally consults a peer cardiologist who exited Sun Pharma earlier (post-IPO) and went heavy into PMS and direct equity; this peer signal influences her exploration of post-ESOP diversification options but doesn't override Amit's recommendations.
