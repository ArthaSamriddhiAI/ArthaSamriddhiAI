## Demographic and Structural Facts

The Malhotras are a dual-professional household at the peak of their accumulation phase. Vikram (47) is a cardiothoracic surgeon with a flagship private practice in Gurgaon's DLF Medanta Mediclinic plus consulting privileges at two other hospitals. Shruti (44) is a senior dermatologist with her own practice plus partial ownership in a chain of 4 cosmetic dermatology clinics across Delhi NCR. Combined practice income: Rs 5.6 Cr/year (Vikram Rs 3.2 Cr, Shruti Rs 2.4 Cr).

They have two children: a 12-year-old son (Class VII, DPS RK Puram) and a 9-year-old daughter (Class IV, same school). Education corpus is a structured priority.

Geographic context: Gurgaon (DLF Phase 4), Metro NCR. Self-occupied home Rs 5.8 Cr (purchased 2014, no loan). Rental property Rs 2.7 Cr (yielding Rs 1.1 L/month). Both are Punjabi-Hindu in cultural context, second-generation urban; financially literate and analytically engaged.

Tax structure: individual filers (not joint family or HUF). Both have HUF Karta status of their respective ancestral HUFs but neither uses the HUF actively for new wealth.

Liquid net worth: Rs 11.8 Cr. Asset class allocation: equity MFs Rs 6.2 Cr (52 percent), debt MFs and tax-free bonds Rs 3.1 Cr (26 percent), FDs Rs 1.5 Cr (13 percent), gold Rs 1 Cr (8 percent). Children's education corpus: Rs 1.2 Cr current within the equity MF total, target Rs 4 Cr by 2036.

## Stated Risk Tolerance and Horizon

Both stated as Aggressive. Stated horizon: 12-15 years until peak retirement (currently age 47 and 44; targeting reduced practice load by mid-50s). Vikram is the more analytically engaged of the two; he reads financial press, follows market commentary, and asks questions. Shruti defers to Vikram on portfolio decisions but reviews summary statements.

Mandate-stated risk tolerance: Aggressive bucket. Mandate-stated horizon: 12-15 years for peak retirement plus 25+ years total.

## Revealed Behavioural Patterns

The Malhotras' revealed pattern matches their stated profile: disciplined and engaged. They have maintained a 12-year SIP across 4 funds (Mirae Largecap, Parag Parikh Flexi Cap, Kotak Emerging Equity, Axis Bluechip) without disruption through 2018-2019 corrections, 2020 COVID dip, 2022 drawdown. They top up SIPs when Vikram's bonus arrives (annual practice bonus pattern; Rs 80L-Rs 1.2 Cr range).

When the 2020 COVID dip hit, Vikram increased the equity SIPs by Rs 50,000/month rather than withdrawing. This was a revealing moment: stated Aggressive plus revealed disciplined-buy-the-dip behaviour aligned cleanly.

Shruti's pattern: more cautious than Vikram. She has questioned (twice in past 24 months) whether the household is over-equity-allocated. Vikram has gently overridden her concerns each time, citing the 12-year horizon. The advisor (Amit Sharma) notes this dynamic; it surfaces in case_arch03_b's scenario discussion (Shruti raised PMS exploration in part because she wanted "more sophistication that we manage actively").

They are responsive to analytical framing. When Amit explains tax efficiency, fee structures, or sectoral reasoning, they engage substantively.

They have never overridden a major advisor recommendation. They have asked thoughtful clarifying questions.

## Key Tension Points

**Tension 1: Vikram's analytical engagement vs Shruti's cautious instinct.** The household's portfolio reflects Vikram's risk preferences; Shruti's quieter conservatism is under-weighted. The PMS scenario (case_arch03_b) is partially Shruti-driven.

**Tension 2: Peak accumulation vs growth ceiling.** They are at Rs 11.8 Cr liquid; trajectory at current accumulation rate puts them at Rs 25-30 Cr by retirement. Their decisions over the next 10 years materially shape that outcome.

**Tension 3: Sophistication readiness vs SIP discipline.** They have demonstrated 12-year SIP discipline. Adding PMS or AIF wrappers introduces complexity. The cost-benefit needs to be clear.

**Tension 4: Children's education corpus tracking.** Currently Rs 1.2 Cr; target Rs 4 Cr by 2036. The household's discretionary deployment must not crowd out the structured education corpus build.
