# Character Bible: The Dharmani Family Office, Mumbai (Archetype 13)

**Status:** Active reference for cluster 6 curation; informs case_arch13_a (PA) and case_arch13_b (BRF).

## Demographic and Structural Facts

The Dharmani Family is a multi-generation business family that has matured into a fee-only family office governance structure. The family's wealth originates from a publicly listed industrial conglomerate (manufacturing, defense, engineering services) founded by the late Naveen Dharmani in 1976. The company listed in 2008; the family progressively reduced its promoter stake from 65 percent to current 11 percent through structured offerings (2010-2022).

The Dharmani Family Office (formal entity established 2018) holds Rs 480 Cr in liquid wealth across diversified holdings. The residual 11 percent stake in the listed company (Rs 280 Cr at current market cap) is held separately; not part of the Rs 480 Cr family office corpus.

The family unit comprises four branches:
- Eldest son Sanjeev Dharmani (61) plus wife Madhuri (58); two children (Karan 32, currently CEO of the listed parent company; Ananya 30, a fashion designer with separate income trajectory)
- Second son Rakesh Dharmani (57) plus wife Anjali (54); two children (Vihaan 28, a Stanford-educated VP at a US tech firm; Aarav 25, MBA-bound)
- Daughter Sunita Dharmani (52) plus husband (separate financial sphere); two children
- Daughter Devika Dharmani (48), unmarried, trustee on multiple family boards

Family office management: Sanjeev is the chair of the family office board; Devika is the trustee-administrator; Rakesh contributes operational consultation. Karan (Gen 3) is observed as future committee member (anticipated within 5-7 years).

Geographic context: Mumbai (Pali Hill, Bandra West). Personal residence and second home: Rs 45 Cr combined (separate from corpus). Cultural context: Marwari-Hindu, traditional business family with progressive governance overlay.

Tax structure: family office holds the corpus through a complex private trust + LLP structure; specialised tax-CA-and-trustee advisory; multi-entity reporting.

Family office AUM Rs 480 Cr composition: listed equity (MFs + direct) Rs 180 Cr; debt + structured credit Rs 90 Cr; REITs and InvITs Rs 60 Cr; international equity via GIFT Rs 40 Cr; private market alternatives across 5 vintages Rs 40 Cr; physical gold + commodity ETFs Rs 40 Cr; SGBs + tax-free bonds Rs 30 Cr; reserved liquidity Rs 40 Cr.

## Stated Risk Tolerance and Horizon

The family office mandate (formalised 2018, refreshed 2023) states: institutional Balanced bucket with multi-vintage private market alternatives; multi-decade stewardship horizon (50+ years across generations).

Stated risk tolerance: institutional preservation-with-growth; private alternatives ceiling 12 percent; international equity ceiling 10 percent; listed equity 35-40 percent; debt + structured credit 18-22 percent; alternatives + REITs/InvITs 12-14 percent; reserved liquidity 8-10 percent.

The family office governance committee meets quarterly. Trustee approval is required for any allocation above Rs 5 Cr; trustee approval is documented and persists.

## Revealed Behavioural Patterns

The Dharmani family office demonstrates institutional discipline at scale. The 5 active Cat II vintages (2018, 2020, 2021, 2023, 2026) reflect structured vintage diversification; capital call schedules are integrated into family office liquidity planning.

They have demonstrated: no panic during 2022 drawdown despite Rs 70 Cr equity drawdown (recovered fully by 2023); structured response to 2018 IL&FS crisis (held high-quality debt; avoided structured products with credit risk); proactive vintage management (2018 Cat II in tail; 2020 in harvesting; 2021 in partial harvesting; 2023 in investment period; 2026 fresh).

They are NOT direct-equity-builders at the family office level (the residual 11 percent stake in the listed parent is held separately). The Rs 110 Cr in Reliance + Rs 100 Cr in TCS + Rs 80 Cr in HDFC Bank were structured allocations from 2014-2017; held since.

International equity (US ETFs via GIFT) was added strategically in 2022; ramped to Rs 40 Cr by 2024.

They are fee-conscious for institutional context but not fee-obsessive. Manager track record evaluation is structured (multi-year window required); they have rejected managers despite favourable short-term returns (in 2024 they rejected a hot-PMS strategy with insufficient track record).

The family office maintains a separate impact-thematic carve-out as an internal experimental allocation (Rs 8 Cr in renewables-themed instruments); not material but signal of family interest.

## Family Dynamics

Sanjeev is the family office chair and de-facto patriarch since the late Naveen's passing (2016). He is present in 100 percent of quarterly meetings and is the principal decision-driver at the committee level.

Rakesh contributes operational sophistication; he is a professional engineer-MBA who served as the listed parent company's CFO (2010-2018). He brings operational rigour to family office discussions.

Devika is the trustee-administrator and brings legal-governance discipline. She manages the Khaitan & Co. relationship and ensures governance documentation standards.

Sunita is the formal observer (her financial sphere is separate from the family office corpus; she is a beneficiary).

Karan (Gen 3, listed parent CEO) is the rising-generation interface; observed at quarterly meetings; anticipated future committee member. He brings a sustainability-themed perspective.

Vihaan (Gen 3, US-based) is non-operational but informed.

The family office has fee-only advisory engagement (Samriddhi via Amit Sharma, with CIO Anjali Mehta directly engaged for committee-level matters). They also retain Khaitan & Co. for legal-trust governance and KPMG for audit.
